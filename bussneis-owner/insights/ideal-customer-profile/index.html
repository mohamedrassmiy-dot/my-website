Who Is Your Customer? Build an Ideal Customer Profile

Expanded from the Business Owner LinkedIn knowledge series into a practical business guide.

Who is your customer?

“Everyone” is usually not a useful answer.

Strong businesses define their Ideal Customer Profile around: • Need — Who has the problem? • Ability — Who can afford the solution? • Authority — Who makes the decision? • Urgency — Who needs it now? • Fit — Who receives the most value?

For B2B, also consider industry, company size, geography, buying process, and decision-makers.

Better targeting improves your message, offer, acquisition cost, and conversion.

Know the customer before trying to scale the customer base.

What this means for a business owner

Use the concept as a decision tool rather than a definition to memorize. Identify where the business currently depends on assumptions, weak economics, unclear ownership or an untested market belief.

Practical application checklist

  • Define need, ability, authority, urgency and fit.
  • Separate user, buyer and decision-maker.
  • Validate assumptions through customer conversations.
  • Align messaging to the highest-value segment.

Turn the framework into action

Choose one measurable business outcome, assign an owner and set a review point. The value of a framework is proven by how it changes execution and results.

Strong businesses turn useful ideas into repeatable operating decisions.
