12 Business Terms Every Founder Should Understand

Expanded from the Business Owner LinkedIn knowledge series into a practical business guide.

From Idea to Sustainable Growth 🚀

12 business terms every founder should understand:

1. Revenue Stream — How your business makes money. 2. MVP — The simplest version used to test market demand. 3. Revenue — Total income generated from sales. 4. Gross Profit — Revenue minus direct costs. 5. Gross Margin — Gross profit as a % of revenue. 6. Net Profit — What remains after all expenses. 7. Profit Margin — Net profit as a % of revenue. 8. Break-Even — When revenue equals total costs. 9. Cash Flow — Cash moving in and out of the business. 10. Burn Rate — How quickly the business spends its cash. 11. CAC — Cost of acquiring one customer. 12. LTV — Value generated by a customer over their lifetime.

The journey:

Idea → MVP → Revenue → Profitability → Cash Management → Customer Economics → Sustainable Growth 📈

Remember:

Revenue ≠ Profit ≠ Cash

And for sustainable customer acquisition:

LTV > CAC

A great idea can start a business. Understanding the numbers helps you build one that lasts.

What this means for a business owner

The important question is not whether the concept sounds correct in theory. The question is what decision it changes inside the business. Use this framework to identify the assumption, bottleneck or economic driver that matters most right now.

Practical application checklist

  • Understand the numbers behind revenue and profit.
  • Track cash separately from accounting profit.
  • Know the metrics that drive the business model.
  • Use financial literacy to improve decisions.

From knowledge to execution

Turn the idea into a defined action: assign an owner, choose a metric, set a review date and compare the result with the original assumption. Strong businesses improve by converting insight into repeatable decisions.

Knowledge creates value when it improves a decision, a process or an outcome.
