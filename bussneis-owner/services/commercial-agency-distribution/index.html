Commercial Agency & Distribution

Brand acquisition strategy, distributor positioning, channel models and go-to-market planning.

When this service is useful

  • Unclear distributor value proposition
  • Choosing the wrong exclusivity model
  • Weak go-to-market plan for an international brand
  • Channel and inventory decisions not linked to market demand

What the engagement focuses on

Distributors and companies seeking international brands or stronger channel growth.

The work begins with the business objective and current constraints. We then identify the highest-leverage issue before recommending tools, channels or structural changes.

Typical outputs

  • Brand and market fit assessment
  • Distributor profile and value proposition
  • Exclusive vs non-exclusive model review
  • Go-to-market and channel growth roadmap

Working approach

Understand → Analyze → Prioritize → Design → Execute → Measure → Improve.

The objective is not to create a large report. It is to improve a decision, system or measurable business outcome.
