## 5. Technical, content, and conversion implementation
- Preserve all existing URLs, approved 301 redirects, canonical and hreflang.
- Add uniquely scoped Arabic and English content to *existing* commercial pages (8 localized URLs); do not create a duplicate article.
- Ensure each page has exactly one H1; summary within the hero; buyer-deliverable sections, accessible visible FAQ, contextual internal links, professional biography and work links, and direct contact CTA.
- Add Service + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage structured data only when it matches visible content; do not use fake AggregateRating, fake reviews or offers.
- Protect private/admin routes and indexability; avoid mass noindex.
- Verify QA: content in build, schema parses, one H1 and canonical per URL, no noindex, relevant bilingual navigation.
- Conversion measurement: use actual verified contact enquiries as outcomes. Do not label a WhatsApp click as a sale.

## 6. Baseline, measurement and acceptance
Track non-branded Saudi queries, impressions, qualified clicks, GSC index coverage, primary landing pages, and verified enquiries over 7/28-day windows after deployment. Google may take weeks to process changes; the deployment and schema do not assure ranking.
